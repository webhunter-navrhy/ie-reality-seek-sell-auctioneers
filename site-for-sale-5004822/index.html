Seek and Sell Auctioneers present this special parcel of land in West Wicklow to the market. Comprising of c.3.11 acres which is nestled in a secluded location. Accessed via private road and surrounded by a mixture of hard and softwood trees. There are stunning views of Lugnaquilla, privacy is in abundance and this opportunity to acquire land of this nature is rare. If unspoilt habitat, views of the Wicklow Mountains and privacy are on your wish list this may be the listing for you.

The land is offered for sale 'as is'. There was planning permission previously granted for a three-bedroom two story residence with garage (planning permission expired). The plans are available upon request. Located just off the R412, just past Tynte Park Cross. C.3.6km to Dunlavin and 9.4km to Grangecon.
